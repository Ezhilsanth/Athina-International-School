About Athina International School

Nurturing Global Ambassadors

Founded in 2019, Athina International School – Kandy (AIS) embarked on its journey under the visionary leadership of Mrs. E. J. Jacob. Guided by our mission to nurture ‘Global Ambassadors for a Sustainable World’, Athina International brings accessible, UK-aligned international standard education to children in Kandy.

Affiliated with Naburn CE Primary School (UK), AIS works closely with educational experts to create an inquiry-based pedagogy. Our vibrant community spans Play School, Pre-School, Primary, and Secondary education (up to Grade 9), supported by dedicated local and foreign educators.
